WHY THIS EXISTS

Less hunting.
More learning.

First year already brings a new campus, new subjects and a different exam pattern. This hub removes one unnecessary problem: figuring out where the good material is.

Every link is grouped by subject and purpose, so juniors can move from syllabus to lecture to revision without opening twenty random tabs.

QUICK ANSWERS

Before you dive in.

Is every resource free?+

Yes. The hub links to freely accessible syllabi, playlists, notes and study resources.

Why do some cards say “coming soon”?+

The original hub does not yet contain a usable link for those items. They are clearly labelled so you never land on a broken page.

Will my saved items appear on another device?+

Not yet. Favourites, tasks and progress are stored privately in your current browser and require no account.
